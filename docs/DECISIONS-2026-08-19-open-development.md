# 2026-08-19 — JOSS pre-review #11136, and the switch to open development

`mentu-navigator` was submitted to JOSS on 2026-08-16 and desk-rejected the
same day (openjournals/joss-reviews#11136). The rejection was correct on
JOSS's criteria: the public repository was created the day of submission,
JOSS requires at least six months of public development history with releases,
issues and pull requests, and the paper lacked four sections JOSS now
requires (State of the field, Software design, Research impact statement,
AI usage disclosure).

Decisions, recorded here so the record is legible:

1. **Open development from this date.** Work lands here, in public, as pull
   requests; releases are tagged; issues are real work items. The pre-2026-08-16
   history was developed privately and published as a cut, which JOSS
   explicitly disfavors — that is not repairable retroactively, only from now.
2. **The software is archived on Zenodo with its own DOI** (distinct from the
   two study deposits it implements), making it citable without a journal.
3. **A JOSS resubmission is possible no earlier than 2027-02-16** and will be
   made only if there is real external adoption to show by then. Activity
   manufactured to look like adoption would be worse than none.
4. The paper in `paper/` now carries the four required sections and repaired
   references, so the record is submission-ready whenever the eligibility
   conditions are actually met.
